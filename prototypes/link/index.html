WhatsApp not connected

No reminders can be sent until WhatsApp is linked.

Link WhatsApp

Use a desktop or laptop to complete this step.

1 Open Respondr in a desktop browser.
2 Go to Link WhatsApp on the desktop.
3 On this phone, open WhatsApp → Settings → Linked devices → Link a device.
4 Scan the QR code shown on the desktop.
5 Pull down on this screen to refresh once linked.
